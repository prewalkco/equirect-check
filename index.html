    Why exactly 2:1?

    An equirectangular image maps 360° horizontally and 180° vertically onto a flat rectangle, so a full sphere is always exactly twice as wide as it is tall: 8192 × 4096, 16384 × 8192 and so on. An image that is close to 2:1 is usually a crop or a partial panorama, and it will look stretched or show a gap at the top or bottom in a 360° viewer.

    Common fixes

    Export from your 360° camera app as a stitched equirectangular image, not a dual-fisheye file. In 3D software, render with a spherical or equirectangular camera and set the output to an exact 2:1 resolution. For top-bottom stereo VR, stack the left and right eye 2:1 images into one 1:1 file.
